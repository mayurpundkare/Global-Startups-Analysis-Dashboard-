# 📌 Project Overview

The **Global Startups Analysis Dashboard** is a Power BI project designed to analyze startup companies across different countries, industries, funding stages, and investment trends. 
This dashboard helps users understand startup growth, funding distribution, market performance, and business expansion globally through interactive visualizations.
The project focuses on transforming raw startup data into meaningful business insights using Power BI analytics and visualization techniques.

# 🎯 Project Objectives
* Analyze startup ecosystems across different countries
* Track funding and investment trends
* Identify top-performing industries and startups
* Visualize global market growth patterns
* Develop interactive dashboards for better decision-making
* Improve data analysis and business intelligence skills using Power BI

# 📊 Dashboard Insights

The dashboard provides insights into:

* Total startups across countries
* Highest funded startups
* Investment trends over years
* Top startup industries
* Country-wise funding distribution
* Startup valuation and growth patterns

# 📈 Key Insights

◆ The United States, India, and the United Kingdom recorded the highest number of startup companies globally.
◆ Technology, FinTech, and E-commerce emerged as the top-performing startup industries with maximum investments.
◆ Startup funding increased significantly over recent years, showing rapid growth in the global startup ecosystem.
◆ Most startups were concentrated in major business hubs and metropolitan cities across different countries.
◆ Several unicorn startups achieved multi-billion-dollar valuations through strong investor support and market expansion.
◆ Seed and Series A funding rounds were the most common investment stages among emerging startups.
◆ Investor analysis showed that venture capital firms heavily invested in AI, FinTech, and SaaS-based startups.
◆ Country-wise analysis highlighted that developed economies attracted higher startup investments compared to developing regions.
◆ The dashboard revealed that startup growth trends varied significantly across industries and geographic locations.
◆ Interactive visualizations helped identify top-funded startups, market trends, and global investment patterns effectively.

# ✅ Conclusion

This Power BI dashboard demonstrates how data visualization and analytics can help understand global startup trends, funding patterns, and business growth across different countries and industries. 
The project enables users to analyze investment distribution, startup performance, and market insights through interactive and meaningful visualizations.
